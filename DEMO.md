# Studivista — Demo Walkthrough

Three roles: **Admin**, **Teacher**, **Student**. Open the app on three devices (or one, logging out in between).

---

## 1. Admin (manages everything)

1. Open app → tap **Admin** → log in with admin credentials.
2. Land on **Admin Dashboard**:
   - 4 stat tiles up top: Teachers / Students / Batches / Classes today.
   - 4 Quick Actions: **Add Teacher**, **Create Batch**, **Add Student**, **View Classes**.
3. Tap **Add Teacher** → enter name, email, password, pick subject → tap **Add Teacher**.
4. Back to dashboard → tap **Add Student** → enter name, email, password → tap **Create Student**.
5. Tap **Create Batch** → name + subject → assign the teacher → schedule days/time → pick a color → tap **Create**.
6. Tap any batch card under **Recent Batches** → **Batch Detail** opens (header, stats, enrolled students).
7. Tap **+ Add Students** → search/multi-select existing students → tap **Add N Students**.
8. Tap the **Teachers** tile (top stats) → see all teachers with online dots and how many batches each has.
9. Tap the **Students** tile → same view for students.

---

## 2. Teacher (runs the class)

1. Log out → tap **Teacher** → log in.
2. **Teacher Dashboard**: today's classes, my batches, my students.
3. Tap **Schedule Class** → pick batch → title, time, duration → tap **Schedule**.
4. In **My Classes**, tap the scheduled class → **Start Class**.
5. Inside the live class:
   - Top: **LIVE** pill, class title, Leave button.
   - Big featured area shows your camera.
   - Tile strip: yourself + students as they join.
   - Bottom bar: **Mute** · **Stop Cam** · **Speaker** · **Share** · **End**.
6. Tap **Share** → Android permission dialog → tap **Start now** → your screen replaces the featured area for everyone.
7. On a student's tile, tap:
   - **Mic icon** → mute that student.
   - **Cam icon** → turn off their camera.
   - **Red X** → remove them from the class.
8. Tap **End** → confirm → class ends, attendance is saved.

---

## 3. Student (joins and participates)

1. Log out → tap **Student** → log in.
2. **Student Dashboard**: live class banner appears at the top when a class is on.
3. Tap **Join Live Class** (or open **Join Class** to see all classes for your batches).
4. In the live class:
   - Big featured area shows the teacher's camera (or screen, when teacher shares).
   - Bottom bar: **Mute** · **Start Cam** · **Speaker** · **Share** · **Leave**.
5. Tap **Share** to share *your* screen if the teacher asks (replaces theirs).
6. Tap **Leave** when done.

---

## Quick concept callouts (mention while demoing)

- **Real-time presence** — green/grey dot on every user shows online status.
- **One-way teacher controls** — teacher can mute or turn off a student's camera; the student can re-enable themselves.
- **One sharer at a time** — when a student starts sharing, the teacher's share auto-stops.
- **Batch-based access** — students in a batch auto-join; others see "Request to Join" and the teacher approves.
